      • Space Play or pause

      • Shift+draw Straight line
      • Cmd/Ctrl+S Save

      How an animation works

      Each frame is a snapshot. Move players on frame 1, press + Frame, move them again, and the board interpolates the movement between the two. Arrows you draw on a frame are revealed as that step plays.
